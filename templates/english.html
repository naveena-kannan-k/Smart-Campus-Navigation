Route Navigation

📍 Route to English Regular Department

Starting Point: Main Gate

1. Go straight for 50 metres

2. Turn right and continue for 50 metres

3. Then continue straight along the road

4. Continue until you reach the Old Auditorium

5. The English Regular Department is located opposite the Old Auditorium, on the first floor

6. Enter the building and go to the first floor to reach the English Regular Department
