Route of Auditorium

📍 Route to Auditorium

Starting Point: Main Gate

1. Walk straight for 70 metres

2. Take a left turn

3. You will find the New Auditorium there

📍 Route to Old Auditorium

Starting Point: Main Gate

1. Walk straight for 50 metres

2. Take a right turn and walk straight for 50 metres

3. Take a left turn

4. You will find the Old Auditorium on the left side
