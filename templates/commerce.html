📍 Route to B.Com Aided Department

Starting Point: Main Gate

1. Walk straight for 50 metres

2. Take a right turn ➡️and walk straight for 40 metres

3. Take a left turn ⬅️ and walk straight for 40 metres

4. Take a left turn ⬅️and enter inside

5. You will find the B.Com Aided Department

6. The B.Com Aided Department is located behind the Management Office
