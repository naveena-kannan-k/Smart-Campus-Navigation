Route Navigation

### 📍 Route to Computer Science Department

**Starting Point: Main Gate**

1. Walk straight for **50 metres**

2. Take a **right turn** and walk straight for **30 metres**

3. Take a **left turn** and walk straight for **50 metres**

4. On the **right side**, you will find the **John Tucker Block**

5. The **Computer Science Department** is located on the **Ground Floor and First Floor** of the John Tucker Block
