📍 Route to Zoology Aided Department

1. Walk straight for 50 metres

2. Take a right turn and walk straight for 40 metres

3. Take a left turn and walk straight for 40 metres

4. Take a left turn

5. The Zoology Aided Department is located behind the Management Office
