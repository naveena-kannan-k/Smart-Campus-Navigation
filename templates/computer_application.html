Computer Application Department

📍 Route to Bachelor of Computer Applications Department

Starting Point: Main Gate

1. Walk straight for 70 metres

2. You will find the New Auditorium

3. Next to the New Auditorium, you will find the Physics SF Block

4. Behind the Physics SF Block, you will find the Bachelor of Computer Applications Department
