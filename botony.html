### 📍 Route to Botany Aided Department

**Starting Point: Main Gate**

1. Walk straight for **50 metres**

2. Take a **right turn**

3. Then take a **left turn**

4. You will reach the **Main Block**

5. In the **Main Block**, the **Botany Aided Department** is located on the **right side of the First Floor**
